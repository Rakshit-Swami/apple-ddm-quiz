## What is this?

A free, browser-based practice quiz to help you revise for the **Apple Certified IT Professional (Apple Deployment & Management)** exam.

All 108 questions are based on topics covered in Apple's own free course:
👉 [Apple Deployment & Management – IT Training](https://it-training.apple.com/deployment/tutorials/course/)

If you haven't done the free course yet — do it first. This quiz is designed to test your knowledge after completing it.

- ✅ 108 questions covering all course topics
- ✅ Questions are randomised every time you start
- ✅ Full review of all answers at the end
- ✅ No login, no ads, no tracking — just open and use

---

## Topics Covered

Based directly on the Apple IT Training course:

- Declarative Device Management (DDM)
- Enrollment methods — ADE, User Enrollment, Device Enrollment
- Apple Business Manager & Apple School Manager
- Apple Configurator for Mac
- Identity, federation & Managed Apple Accounts
- Network — Wi-Fi, VPN, 802.1X, content caching
- Software updates & OS version management
- Apps & Books — VPP, Custom Apps, distribution
- Security — FileVault, Activation Lock, smart cards, Secure Enclave
- Restrictions, commands & device queries
